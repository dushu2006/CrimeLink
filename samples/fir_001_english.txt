FIRST INFORMATION REPORT
Police Station: M.I. Road, Jaipur City (Rajasthan)
FIR No.: 231/2024                                   Date: 14/08/2024
Act & Sections: 384, 386, 120B, 420 IPC

1. Statement of complainant Smt. Sunita Devi, wife of Shri Kailash Chand,
   resident of Bapu Nagar, Jaipur, aged about 46 years.

2. On 13/08/2024 at about 21:30 hours, three persons came to my garment shop
   situated near M.I. Road, Jaipur and demanded Rs 5,00,000 as protection money
   (rangdari tax), threatening that refusal would result in damage to the shop
   and harm to my family.

3. On enquiry from the adjoining plot holders I learnt that the accused
   Ramesh Kumar Yadav alias Ramesh Yadav, son of Shri Ram Prasad Yadav, resident
   of Sanganer, Jaipur, along with Suresh Mehta and Vikram Singh Rathore, had
   been threatening other traders of the same market for the past two months.
   Suresh Mehta is a known associate of Ramesh Kumar Yadav and collects the
   money on his behalf. Vikram Singh Rathore is the brother of Meena Rathore and
   was earlier arrested along with Ramesh Kumar Yadav in case FIR 118/2022 of
   Police Station Kota City.

4. Accused Ramesh Kumar Yadav was driving a white Mahindra Scorpio bearing
   registration no. RJ 14 AB 4321. He was accompanied by Suresh Mehta at the
   time of the threat.

5. The complainant subsequently received threatening calls from mobile numbers
   9811023456 and 9829012345. The caller directed that the amount be
   transferred to account number 50100234567890 maintained with the Sanganer
   branch (IFSC SBIN0001234). The complainant transferred Rs 48,000 as a first
   instalment on 14/08/2024 through NEFT.

6. The accused persons are members of an organised gang operating across Jaipur
   and Kota and are believed to be acting in furtherance of a common intention.
   One Anil Sharma, proprietor of Sharma Traders, Jaipur, is suspected of
   routing the collected money.
